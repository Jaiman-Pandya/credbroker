What this is

CredBroker sits between AI agents and real APIs such as Google Drive. Agents never receive OAuth credentials. They receive short lived signed passes and the broker makes the actual call on their behalf. Credentials stay encrypted inside the broker and every action lands in an audit log. This console is the operator surface for the broker running behind this page.

How to use it

Press GO on step 1 of the operating procedure and follow the strip left to right. Every button performs a real call. Watch the diagram light as current flows and watch the annunciator wall fill. The desks further down let you drive everything by hand.
